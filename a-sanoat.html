Akril va akril aralashmali ip ishlab chiqarishda O'zbekistonning eng yirik ishlab chiqaruvchisi bo'lgan ASL IP Markaziy Osiyoning yetakchi korxonalaridan biridir. Jami 18 mamlakatga eksport qilib, O'zbekistondagi akril va akril aralashmali ipga bo'lgan ehtiyojning 45 foizini ASL IP qondiradi.

ASL IP zamonaviy ishlab chiqarish quvvatlari bilan sohadagi mavjud sifatlarni ishlab chiqarish bilan birga, bozor ehtiyojlarini hisobga olib, narx ustunligini taklif etadi va yangi mahsulotlar yaratish ustida ishlab, sohaning yetakchi korxonasi hisoblanadi.

ASL IP, the largest producer of acrylic and acrylic-blend yarn in Uzbekistan, is also among the leading producers in Central Asia. Exporting to 18 countries in total, ASL IP meets 45 percent of Uzbekistan's demand for acrylic and acrylic-blend yarn.

With state-of-the-art production facilities, ASL IP not only spins the qualities the sector already knows but watches what the market needs, offers a price advantage and keeps developing new products, which makes it the sector's pioneering company.

Laboratoriya nazoratiLaboratory control

Har bir partiya ip nomeri, burama soni, uzilish kuchi va cho'zilish bo'yicha tekshiriladi. Bo'yalgan iplar yuvish, ishqalanish va yorug'likka chidamlilik testlaridan o'tadi, natijalar sertifikat bilan yetkaziladi.

Every lot is checked for count, twist, tenacity and elongation. Dyed yarns pass washing, rubbing and light-fastness tests, and the results ship with the goods as a certificate of analysis.
